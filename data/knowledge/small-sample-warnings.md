# Small Sample Warnings

A backtest with few completed trades is exploratory, not confirmatory.

## Rules of thumb

- Very small samples (for example under 30 trades) are sensitive to a few outliers.
- Parameter choice (threshold, lookback, holding period) can change conclusions.
- No out-of-sample or walk-forward validation means results may not generalize.
- Regime dependence: bull, bear, and sideways markets can dominate the sample.

## Product language

Prefer plain-language warnings over fake confidence scores. State what the data shows, what remains uncertain, and what to test next.
